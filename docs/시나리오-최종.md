## 1. 내레이션 (TTS에 한 줄씩 붙여넣기)

```text
N01 | Alex is staying in Seoul. A while ago, they saved a photo of a city notice they can't fully read. Details change. Is that photo still right?
N02 | Alex sets English as the display language. Their region stays at home. StillValid never infers it from language, and uses location only if Alex asks.
N03 | Alex uploads the photo. Meta's Muse Spark model reads it into structured details: title, organiser, edition and dates, keeping the original wording.
N04 | The notice is Korean, so Alex selects Korea. Their region is the US. StillValid flags the mismatch instead of silently mixing them up.
N05 | Alex adds the official notice page. Without one, Meta's web search can suggest candidates.
N06 | [침묵 · 효과음만]
N07 | The deadline moved, from March 31st on the card to April 3rd on the official page. We altered the card's date for this demo, and StillValid caught it. Both exact lines appear side by side, in the original Korean. Our own code re-checks that each quote really appears there, on a registered Seoul government domain. And it only vouches for what it checked, right now.
N08 | A similar page from a different year won't count as a change. A wrong confirmation is worse than asking you to check again.
N09 | Switch languages; the verdict and evidence stay the same.
N10 | Copy a summary to share. StillValid doesn't keep the original photo, results expire after 24 hours, and you can delete them anytime.
N11 | Built on Meta's Muse Spark, with deterministic checks on every quote. StillValid: before you act on a saved notice, check that it's still valid.
```

TTS 메모: 차분한 중립 음성 1개, 48kHz WAV, 줄마다 따로 생성. 발음 확인 *StillValid*, *Muse Spark*, *Seoul*. 길이가 구간을 넘으면 문장을 줄이고 배속하지 않습니다. 실존 인물 목소리 복제 금지.

```text

Compare — Muse Spark compares field by field with exact quotes
Verify — deterministic checks: quotes, registered domain, title, institution, edition, section
Result — matched / changed / needs another look
```

## 5. 유튜브 설명란 (복사용)

```text
StillValid — Saved a notice. Still the same?
https://stillvalid-beta.vercel.app

StillValid compares a saved notice photo with the official page and shows what changed, with the exact quotes from both. When it can't prove it's the same notice, it doesn't confirm.

About this video
- Narration and motion graphics were generated with AI tools. The app screens are real recordings of the live production app in a mobile-browser viewport (build f60f6fe, Nov 4, 2026); the live app has since been updated.
- The card in the demo is a reconstruction of a real public Seoul Metropolitan Government notice. We deliberately changed its application deadline (31 March) so the app could detect the difference from the official page (3 April). This does not mean the institution changed a deadline; the notice closed in April 2026.
- The "different edition" example is a built-in synthetic example with no live lookup.
- The same input can also end in "needs another look". In our recording session the change was confirmed in 2 of 3 checks. Accuracy has not been measured on a human-labelled real-notice set.
- The current deployment uses Meta's muse-spark-1.3-contributor model: what you submit may be used to improve Meta's models. Don't submit anything you wouldn't want shared.
- Photos are re-encoded in the browser, StillValid does not keep the original image file, and results expire after 24 hours and can be deleted.

Built with Meta Muse Spark (reading, search, comparison) and optional SAM 3.1, plus deterministic evidence checks.
Code: https://github.com/ZOOGOM/stillvalid
```
